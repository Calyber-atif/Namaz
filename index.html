How Namaz Lock works

Three quiet steps. No shame, ever.

Namaz Lock never scolds you. It simply stands between you and the scroll at the moments that matter most.

◷
Step one

Choose your windows

Pick which of the five prayers Namaz Lock watches over, and which apps should wait. Start with one — starting small is starting.

🔒
Step two

The apps wait

When the window opens, Instagram can wait. You get a gentle gate — an ayah, the rakah count, and the guide if you need it.

﴾
Step three

Read one ruku

Read a single ruku — guided through the 558 toward a full khatm, or freely wherever your heart is. Then your phone is yours again.

A living home screen

The sky moves with your prayers.

From the pale hush of Fajr to the deep indigo of Isha, Makkah sits at the top of your day — sun arcing, birds crossing, the light shifting with every window. It's a reminder you'll actually want to look at.
